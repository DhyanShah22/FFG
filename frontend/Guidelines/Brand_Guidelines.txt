Antarang Foundation Design System

You are building a modern, accessible web platform for Antarang Foundation, an NGO helping students discover careers and make informed education and career decisions.

The website should feel:

Hopeful
Human
Empowering
Modern
Trustworthy
Youthful
Clean
Warm
Optimistic

Avoid anything that feels corporate, flashy, startup-like, or overly playful.

Overall Design Language

Think of the interface as a combination of:

Duolingo's optimism
Notion's cleanliness
Stripe's spacing
Government accessibility standards
NGO storytelling

The UI should prioritize clarity over decoration.

Lots of whitespace.

Rounded corners (12-20px).

Soft shadows only where necessary.

Large typography.

Friendly layouts.

Never cluttered.

Important Constraints

Always follow these rules:

Only use the approved brand colors.
Use Roboto throughout the application.
Use rounded components and generous whitespace.
Keep layouts simple and easy to scan.
Use authentic student-focused imagery.
Never use loud gradients or neon colors.
Accent colors (lime and yellow) should only highlight important information or calls to action.
Every screen should feel optimistic, calm, and welcoming.
If a design decision is ambiguous, prioritize accessibility, clarity, and emotional warmth over visual complexity.

This design system should be applied consistently across all pages, components, and future additions to ensure the platform reflects Antarang Foundation's mission of empowering young people through career guidance. It aligns with the foundation's official brand guidance on colors, typography, imagery, visual style, and communication principles
